## Reasoning in Plain English

This incident affects more than one staff member and impacts access to an important school service. Similar previous incidents involving multiple users after password resets were treated as P2 incidents and were linked to identity or authentication synchronisation delays.

Because the issue affects access to lesson resources and attendance information, it should not be treated as a simple single-user password issue.
